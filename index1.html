Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the session?

30 minutes. The session is focused and efficient while still giving you time to relax, get comfortable, and create beautiful images.

How many images do I receive?

Five fully retouched images of your choice. You'll select your favorites from your gallery after the session.

Can I purchase additional images?

Yes. Your five images are included. If you fall in love with additional photographs, you'll have the opportunity to purchase them separately.

Do I need to know how to pose?

Absolutely not. That's part of what you're hiring us for. We'll guide you through poses, movement, expression and positioning throughout your session.

What should I wear?

For a Flying Dress Mini Session, your dress becomes part of the experience. We'll guide you through movement and posing so you don't have to worry about knowing what to do.

What happens if the weather doesn't cooperate?

We'll work with you to determine whether conditions are suitable for your session and make adjustments when necessary.
